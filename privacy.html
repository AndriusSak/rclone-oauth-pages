Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

What this application does

A personal OAuth client, used by the rclone command-line tool running on the owner's own machine, copies local backup archives into a single folder in the owner's own Google Drive account.

What data it accesses

Only files that the owner uploads through rclone, inside one folder chosen by the owner. Files are encrypted on the owner's machine before upload, so Google stores opaque ciphertext.

What is collected or shared

Nothing. No analytics, no advertising, no third-party services, no data sold or shared. The application has one user: its owner.

Data retention and deletion

Data lives in the owner's Google Drive and is governed by the owner's own retention settings. Deleting files from the Drive folder, or revoking the application's access, removes the application's ability to reach them.

Contact

Questions can be raised through the owner's public GitHub profile.
